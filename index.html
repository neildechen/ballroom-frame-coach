Frame Coach

Live check of your Standard frame and head, measured against your own sway.

  1. Prop the phone at chest height, 2 to 3 metres away, facing you.
  2. Hips in view for full checks. Waist-up works too, using the shoulder line as level.
  3. Take hold. Red means that side or the head is off.
